Frequently asked

I lost my progress / my battles are locked again. What happened?

Tapping Restart Campaign from inside the app wipes your save file. So does uninstalling and reinstalling the app. The save file lives only on your device — there is no cloud sync.

Can I move my save to another device?

Not at this time. The app stores progress locally only, with no accounts or cloud sync. iOS device-to-device transfer (via Quick Start or iCloud restore) will carry the save over if you set up the new device from the old one's backup; otherwise it starts fresh.

The app crashed / a battle seems unwinnable.

Please email ryanevans23@gmail.com with the sea and battle number and what happened. Crashes are very rare — the app makes zero network requests, has no third-party SDKs, and is pure HTML5/Canvas, so most issues are reproducible bugs that get patched quickly.

Will there be more battles / seas?

Probably. v1.0 ships with 120 free battles across 12 seas, plus the optional Cursed Tides pack (50 battles in five cursed seas). More seas may follow if there's interest.

I bought a boost pack but didn't get the boosts.

Boost packs are consumable in-app purchases processed by Apple. In the rare case a purchase completes but the boosts don't appear, fully close and reopen the app — an interrupted transaction finishes on the next launch. If you were charged and still don't see the boosts, email me with the transaction ID from your Apple receipt and I'll sort it out. (Consumable boosts can't be re-granted by Restore Purchases — that button restores the Cursed Tides pack, not used-up boosts.)

I bought the Cursed Tides pack on another device / after reinstalling.

Open the Treasury (tap any battle, then Get More Boosts) and tap Restore Purchases. The pack is tied to your Apple ID, so restoring it is always free.

Is there an Android version?

Not currently. The app is built with Capacitor so an Android port is technically straightforward; it's a question of time and demand. Email if you want one — interest signals matter.
